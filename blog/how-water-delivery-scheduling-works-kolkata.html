How Water Delivery Scheduling Works in Kolkata: One-Time Orders, Slots, and Recurring Plans

Quick answer: Kolkata water delivery works three ways — a one-time order (booked today, delivered on the next route day), a scheduled refill (the same slot weekly or fortnightly, so you never run out), or an office roster (fixed multi-jar deliveries with a single invoice). You book any of them on WhatsApp in about two minutes; the jar deposit and exchange happen at your door on the first delivery.

By Maharani Beverages LLP — Operations Team · Published 2 November 2026

Most first-time customers ask the same things: do I have to order every week? What if I'm not home? What happens to my empty jar? This is the plain guide to how scheduling actually works — the same process our delivery team follows across Kolkata and Howrah.

Option 1: One-time orders

Message us on WhatsApp with your address and the number of jars. We slot you into the next delivery route for your area — typically within a day or two — and confirm the window before the van leaves. One-time orders suit guests, events, and trying the service before committing to a schedule.

Option 2: Scheduled refills (the household standard)

Tell us your weekly consumption and we fix a recurring slot — say, every Monday and Thursday morning. The same route covers your building, so the driver learns the address, the gate, and where you want jars left if you're out. You can pause for holidays with one message. This is how most households stop thinking about water: it simply arrives.

Option 3: Office and commercial rosters

Offices, clinics, and shops usually move to a roster: fixed delivery days, multiple jars, and a single invoice cycle instead of per-drop payments. Dispenser points get priority on the route. If you manage an office pantry, mention headcount and dispenser count on WhatsApp and we'll propose a roster that never leaves the cooler empty on a Monday.

What happens on delivery day

The driver arrives in the confirmed window, collects your empty jars (exchange for exchange), and hands over sealed, date-coded jars. Check two things at the door: the cap seal is intact, and the batch date is recent. That two-second check is your quality assurance — and it's the first thing our label guide teaches you to inspect.

Jar deposit and exchange

Returnable 20 litre jars carry a refundable deposit, exchanged jar-for-jar on every delivery. Exact deposit amounts and any minimums depend on your order pattern — ask on WhatsApp and we'll state everything in writing before your first delivery, so there are no surprises.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can I get my first delivery in Kolkata?

Usually on the next route day for your area — typically within one to two days of booking. Urgent requirements are accommodated where routes allow; tell us the date and we'll confirm honestly.

What if I am not home during the delivery window?

Leave empties at the agreed spot and tell us where to place the sealed jars — most households use a balcony, lobby, or the security desk. The driver follows your standing instructions once set.

Can I pause scheduled deliveries?

Yes — one WhatsApp message pauses your slot for holidays or changed usage, and restarts it just as easily. No penalties.

How do office rosters and billing work?

Fixed delivery days, agreed jar counts, and a single invoice cycle instead of per-delivery payments. Mention staff and dispenser counts for a roster proposal.
