# 14 — AI Search and Discovery Strategy

**Date:** 2026-11-01 · **Posture:** fundamentals-first; no speculative GEO tactics (per brief Part XIV). Context [O]: Google's AI Overviews now appear in a large share of searches (reported ~43% in 2026); local supplier discovery is increasingly answer-mediated.

## How buyers may discover water suppliers via AI answers

A user asks an AI assistant "who delivers 20 litre water jars in Salt Lake?" The answer is assembled from: entity understanding (does Google know this business exists, where, with what services) + corroboration (consistent citations across GBP, directories, press, structured site data) + content that directly answers the question in citable form. Zenith's readiness assessment:

| Readiness factor | State | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Crawlable, structured site | ✅ (1,416 valid JSON-LD, clean architecture) | — |
| Entity consistency (name/phone/licence/address family) | ✅ (entity audit 2026-11-01) | exact NAP via GBP/citations pending |
| Direct-answer content (answer-first pattern) | ✅ 3 authority articles + quick answers | extend per editorial calendar |
| Unique citable assets (lab reports, verification checklists) | 🔴 not live | lab library (owner docs) |
| Third-party corroboration | 🔴 none live | Month-2 citations + Month-3 PR |
| GBP presence | 🔴 not verified | Month-2 (owner) |

## Strategy (defensible actions only)

1. **Answer-first everywhere** (already the house pattern): definitional questions get a citable 2–3 sentence answer in the first paragraph — the label guide, can-vs-jar piece, and IS 14543 article all do this. Continue per calendar.
2. **Entity completion** — GBP + the 6 citations make Zenith's entity unambiguous across the surfaces AI systems corroborate from. Highest-leverage AI-readiness action, already P0.
3. **Citable assets** — lab-report library and the verification checklist are link-and-citation magnets; when third parties cite them, AI answers gain a source beyond our own site.
4. **Consistent facts** — every public surface states the same licence number, address family, and coverage. The entity audit becomes a quarterly gate.
5. **Q&A-shaped content** — the FAQ pattern (visible + schema 1:1) matches how questions are asked conversationally; the editorial calendar prioritises question-form topics.

## Explicitly rejected

No AI-schema inventions · no fake third-party mentions · no "optimise for ChatGPT" doorway pages · no unverified claims to appear in generated answers · no llms.txt theatre without evidence it matters.

## Measurement

Monthly: 10 fixed question probes (e.g., "water delivery Salt Lake Kolkata", "is Zenith Water FSSAI licensed", "20 litre jar price Kolkata") run through available AI surfaces; presence/absence + accuracy logged in `14-future-search-opportunity-register.csv`. Trend, not snapshots.
