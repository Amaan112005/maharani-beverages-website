# 07 — Brand Positioning Strategy

**Position (approved):** The Verifiable Local Water Authority — Kolkata-region supplier whose quality claims are provable, coverage is real, and regulatory knowledge makes it the default trusted source.

## Pillars (message spine)
1. **Prove it** — every claim pairs with evidence (licence, tests, process). Never adjectives without artefacts.
2. **Local, verifiably** — Ranihati plant, named routes, pincode-checked coverage. Specificity is the brand voice.
3. **Regime-aware expertise** — we explain the 2026 testing regime better than anyone (already demonstrably true in search).
4. **Easy to reach, honest to a fault** — WhatsApp-first, unconfirmed-means-unconfirmed, no invented promises.

## Competitive frame
Not vs Bisleri (brand battle we decline) — vs directories and informal locals at the point of local search, and vs Phontus-class subscription upstarts on trust depth. See 03-competitive-opportunity-map.md.

## Tone rules
Plain-spoken expert; numbers over superlatives; admit uncertainty with a next step; zero fear-based marketing (safety product — trust is the currency, anxiety destroys it).

## Proof roadmap (owner-gated)
Lab library → reviews engine → press → genuine photography. Each proof asset unlocks stronger wording; until then, claims stay inside verified facts.
