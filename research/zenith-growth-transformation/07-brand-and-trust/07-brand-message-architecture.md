# 07 — Brand Message Architecture

| Layer | Message | Proof point | Where used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand promise | "Safe water, honestly delivered — check us before you buy." | verification content + licence | homepage hero rotation, compliance |
| Quality | "FSSAI-licensed, tested monthly under the 2026 Scheme of Testing." | licence 12825999000938; lab library (gated) | products, FAQ, articles |
| Local | "Made at Ranihati, delivered across {city} on real routes." | plant identity, pincode checker, coverage | city/locality pages |
| Commercial | "Homes, offices, hotels — quoted clearly, supplied on schedule." | visible 20L tiers, segment flows | commercial pages |
| Distribution | "A distributorship you can verify — no application fees, ever." | Udyam + FSSAI on page, documents | distributor page |
| Voice constants | "Zenith Water" (brand) / "Maharani Beverages LLP" (legal) exact; phone +91 82748 37341; licence number verbatim | entity audit | everywhere |
| Prohibited | "purest/safest/best in India", health promises, unverified competitor claims, pressure tactics | quality standard §6 | editorial + ad gate |

**Message-to-claim discipline:** any wording stronger than this table requires a register row + evidence. Owner approves deviations (G2 gate).
