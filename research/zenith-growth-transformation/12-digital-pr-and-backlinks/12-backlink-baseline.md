## How to establish the real baseline (owner, ~15 minutes)

1. Verify the site in Google Search Console (if not already) → Links report.
2. Record: total external links, total referring domains, top linked pages, top linking sites — export monthly.

4. Archive exports in `data/` with dates — month-over-month trend is the metric, not any single snapshot.
