# 02 — Customer Interview Framework (for the owner-conducted sessions)

**Purpose:** replace [H]-class persona assumptions with observed behaviour, per Phase 2. **Conducted by:** owner/sales team (they have customer access; we do not). **Output:** answers appended to the business-facts register + persona updates. Never fabricate results — unanswered questions stay [U].

## Rules

- Ask about **past behaviour** ("what did you do last time"), not intentions ("would you consider…").
- No leading questions; silence is fine; note exact words (customer language feeds copy).
- 20–30 minutes each; record with consent; 5–8 interviews per segment is enough for directional signal.

## Question sets

### Existing household customers
1. How did you first find your current water supplier? (probe: search / neighbour / delivery van / shop)
2. What made you choose them over others?
3. What did you need to know before your first order? What did you ask?
4. Walk me through your last order — how did it happen, start to finish?
5. What, if anything, was frustrating about that?
6. Have you ever changed water suppliers? What triggered it?
7. How do you judge whether the water/jar is trustworthy?
8. What would make you recommend a supplier to a neighbour?

### Office admins (ideally 2 who chose Zenith, 1 who didn't)
1. Who handles water supply decisions at your office, and how often does it come up?
2. What does a supplier need to provide for you to say yes — invoice, dispenser, roster?
3. What's the failure you fear most (stock-out on Monday, billing disputes…)? Has it happened?
4. How did you find your current supplier? What did you compare?

### Hotels/restaurants procurement
1. What documentation do you need from a water supplier for your FSSAI audits?
2. What has gone wrong with water suppliers before?
3. How are trade terms usually structured — credit, delivery frequency?

### Retailers
1. How do you decide which water brand to stock?
2. What margin and delivery frequency do you expect?

### Distributors/prospects
1. What territory economics make a distributorship viable? (margins, volumes)
2. What made you cautious about distributorship offers? (fraud awareness)
3. What proof of legitimacy do you look for?

### Lost leads / enquiries that didn't convert (if reachable)
1. What were you looking for that day?
2. What happened after you enquired? (response speed, answer quality)
3. What made you choose the alternative?

## Analysis template

Per interview: segment · triggers · decision criteria · objections · exact phrases used · trust evidence cited. Across interviews: frequency-ranked findings update the persona DB and the objection matrix; contradictions with current site copy become change requests.
