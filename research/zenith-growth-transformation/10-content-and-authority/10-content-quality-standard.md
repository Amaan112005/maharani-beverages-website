# Content Quality Standard (editorial gate — all publications)

1. **Audience first** — every piece names its audience and the question it answers in the brief; no content produced to increase page count.
2. **Answer-first structure** — the direct answer sits in the first 100 words (citable form for search + AI surfaces).
3. **Fact discipline** — regulatory claims only from verified sources (Phase-1 timeline; official FSSAI/BIS documents at publication); business facts only from owner-confirmed register rows. Unverified = [U] = does not publish.
4. **G3 gate** — any regulatory wording change passes the Phase-1 review check before shipping.
5. **Evidence classes inline** in research docs; marketing copy itself cites sources where claims are load-bearing (licence numbers, dates, standards).
6. **No prohibited claims** — "purest/safest/best in India" and health promises without substantiation; competitor disparagement; fabricated reviews/testimonials/quotes.
7. **1:1 schema-visible parity** — FAQ/HowTo markup mirrors visible content exactly.
8. **Internal links with intent** — 2–4 contextual links to the most relevant commercial pages; descriptive anchors in the customer's everyday register.
9. **Byline + review date** — real organisational author; visible review date refreshed on substantive updates.
10. **Conversion posture** — soft CTA on informational content; hard CTA only where intent is commercial. Never two competing CTAs per viewport.
11. **Refresh duty** — quarterly sweep of dates, statistics, and links on the top-20 traffic pages.
12. **Original value** — every post adds something no competitor page offers (process transparency, checklists, verified data) — the differentiation test.
