## A. Why people buy packaged drinking water (motivations)

Tier-1 research anchor: Doria (2006, PubMed, 637 citations) found consumer surveys consistently identify **two dominant drivers**: dissatisfaction with tap water **organoleptics (taste/odour)** and **health/risk concerns** [R]. A 2026 Siliguri (West Bengal) municipal study found safety and taste concerns drive the shift to bottled water **irrespective of income level** [R] — directly transferable to Zenith's market.

India bottled water demand is growing at ~10.2% CAGR (FY2024–2031, MarketsandData) [R], so the category tailwind is real.

Motivation map by segment [R+H]:

| Motivation | Household | Office | Hotel/Rest. | Retailer | Distributor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Health/safety perception | ● primary | ● primary | ● primary | ○ | ○ |
| Taste | ● | ● | ● (affects food) | ○ | – |
| Convenience of delivery | ● | ● | ● | – | – |
| Price | ● sensitive | ○ | ○ | ● margin | ● margin |
| Trust in brand | ● | ●● (liability) | ●● (FSSAI audits) | ●● (repeat resale) | ●● (brand risk) |
| Availability/reliability | ● | ●● (never run out) | ●● | ●● stock continuity | ●● supply continuity |
| Recurring need automation | ● subscription | ●● rostered | ● | – | – |
| Emergency requirement | ● episodic | ○ | ● events | – | – |

What differs: households buy **risk-reduction for family**; offices buy **operational continuity** ("never run out on Monday morning"); hospitality buys **audit-proof compliance**; retailers/distributors buy **margin + supply reliability** [H, consistent with JalYantra's operator research: the business is "100% local, 100% word-of-mouth", density beats reach [O]].

## C. Decision psychology — behavioural economics applied (not blindly)

| Principle | How it applies to a water purchase |
|---|---|
| Loss aversion | "Never run out" framing beats "get water" — office churn is triggered by a stock-out, remembered 2x stronger than a smooth delivery [H from prospect theory, validated by subscription query existing in GSC: "water subscription" 82 impr] |
| Social proof | Decisive for an unknown local brand; reviews/photos of real deliveries are the strongest trust proxy. Note: fabricated review markup was REMOVED in the relaunch — the correct remedy is REAL reviews on GBP/WhatsApp/Justdial, referenced visibly |
| Authority | FSSAI licence number + (if obtainable) BIS ISI mark + lab reports. Authority signals are the #1 differentiator vs informal suppliers [R, F] |
| Familiarity/mere exposure | Repeat delivery branding on jars/caps is a real-world retargeting loop [H] |
| Cognitive load | One CTA per page section; the near-me visitor needs "available in your area + price + WhatsApp" within one viewport — measured in deliverable 08 |
| Decision fatigue | Pricing tiers kept to 3 (household/office/bulk) — already implemented on 20L page |
| Perceived risk | Free first delivery / sample jar reduces trial risk [H — validate margin with owner] |
| Friction reduction | WhatsApp deep link with PRE-FILLED intent text cuts enquiry friction to 2 taps; implemented site-wide, refined per-segment below |
| Commitment/consistency | Subscription nudge AFTER first successful delivery, not before [H] |

## E. Personas (research-grounded; hypotheses labelled)

### P1 — Household decision-maker (homemaker or young professional, Kolkata/Howrah apartment)
- Motivation: family safety + convenience [R]; Search: near-me + jar terms on mobile (mobile = 82.6% of site clicks [F]); Objections: trust, price, jar hygiene [R/H]; Trust needs: FSSAI number visible, real reviews, locality familiarity; Frequency: weekly/bi-weekly; Channel: WhatsApp; Conversion: WhatsApp order with pincode-aware message; **Preventers: price opacity, no delivery confirmation, slow reply.** GSC anchor: "20 litre water supply near me" (686 impr, pos 5.5) [F].

### P2 — Office administrator (30–50, orders for 10–200 staff)
- Motivation: continuity + zero complaints [H]; Search: "office water supply kolkata", "bulk water supply" (b2b cluster: 83 impr [F]); Objections: invoice/billing, roster reliability; Trust: GST invoice, corporate references; Frequency: daily/weekly recurring; Channel: form + WhatsApp; Conversion: bulk quote with rostered delivery. **Preventers: no pricing signal, no billing info.**

### P3 — Small business owner (shop/salon/clinic)
- Between P1 and P2; price-sensitive; buys on WhatsApp after referral [O — word-of-mouth dominance].

### P4 — Retail shopkeeper
- Motivation: margin + stock continuity; Search: rarely via web (B2B discovery via IndiaMART/Justdial [O]); Trust: brand legitimacy (resale risk); Conversion: distributor contact. Site serves as verification asset more than acquisition asset [H].

### P5 — Hotel/restaurant purchasing manager
- Motivation: audit-proof compliance, consistent taste [R]; Search: "packaged drinking water for hotels kolkata"; Trust: FSSAI + BIS documentation, consistent batch quality; Objection: failed audit = business risk; Conversion: B2B enquiry with documentation attached. **Preventers: missing compliance documentation on site.**

### P6 — Event organiser
- Episodic, urgent, volume-heavy ("event water supply kolkata" page exists); Search window: 3–10 days pre-event; Conversion: fast quote. **Preventers: no instant quote, no capacity signal.**

### P7 — Bulk procurement manager (factory/institution)
- RFP-style process; desktop-heavy (desktop pos 11.6 vs mobile 7.1 [F] suggests under-served); Needs: specs, capacity, credit terms; Conversion: documented quote.

### P8 — Prospective distributor
- Motivation: territory economics; Search: "water distributorship", brand+distributorship; Trust: company legitimacy, exclusivity, supply reliability; **Bisleri's fraud warning proves this audience is actively targeted by scams — a verified, transparent distributor page is a differentiator [O].**

## F. What makes people switch suppliers / stay loyal [H, consistent with O-sources]
Switch triggers: stock-out, price hike without notice, jar hygiene incident, rude delivery staff, unanswered phone. Loyalty drivers: never-run-out reliability + relationship with the delivery person + subscription inertia. **Fast WhatsApp response is the single most controllable conversion factor** [H — measurable via GA4 whatsapp_click→lead tracking once GA4 lands].
