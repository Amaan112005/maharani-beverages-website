## 1. Market frame [R]

- India bottled water market CAGR ~10.2% FY2024–2031 (MarketsandData).
- Category demand anchored by tap-water taste/safety dissatisfaction (Doria 2006; Siliguri 2026 study) — structural, not fashion.
- Regulatory temperature rising: FSSAI classified packaged drinking water as **high-risk** (Dec 2024) with mandatory pre-licence inspections; industry sources consistently state BIS certification under IS 14543 is the legal licence to sell packaged drinking water (aimtechindia 2026: "you need both, they govern different things"). **Conflict note:** Zenith's locality-page FAQ states "BIS certification is no longer mandatory as of 2026; FSSAI's testing scheme applies" — multiple 2026 industry sources contradict this. **[U] — owner must verify with counsel; if the site claim is wrong it is both a compliance and trust exposure (see Risk R1).**
- Delivery economics (JalYantra operator research): the business is 100% local, word-of-mouth; density beats reach. This validates Zenith's Kolkata/Howrah concentration over thin national plays.

## 3. Competitor website findings

### Bisleri (bisleri.com, fetched 2026-10-31) [F/O]
- Positioning: "India's Most Trusted Name in Drinking Water"; 10-step purification, 90 quality tests; **130 plants, 5,980 distributors, 8,270 trucks**.
- Conversion: e-commerce + **Bisleri@Doorstep app with subscription**; no WhatsApp; no visible pricing; **no locality landing pages** (national template site).
- Trust assets: decades of brand equity + "Bottles for Change" CSR programme (34,888 MT recycled, 10M citizens) + FAQ content.
- Notable: **permanent fraud warning about fake distributorships** — evidence that the distributor-acquisition journey is contested territory.
- **Gap Zenith can exploit:** zero local SEO, no transparent pricing, no WhatsApp channel, no Kolkata presence in search.

### Kinley / Coca-Cola [O]
- "Trust in Every Drop" (est. 2000); retail-led; Coke2Home delivery limited to Delhi NCR; wholesale via IndiaMART listings. No local landing pages. Same gaps as Bisleri plus weaker direct-commerce story.

### Local Kolkata suppliers (via Justdial/Sulekha observations) [O]
- Discovered competitors: Proyojani, Samaria Food & Beverage, EME Water Delivery App, Arup Enterprise, Venlite, plus ~dozens on directory category pages.
- Their web presence = directory listings with ratings (Justdial reviews visible). **Zenith's website is already deeper than any observed local competitor's own site** — but Zenith is invisible where the local decision happens: directories + GBP map pack.

## 6. Strategic conclusions

1. **Do not fight Bisleri as a brand — fight as a local service.** Bisleri wins "packaged water brand" searches; Zenith wins "water delivery near me / 20 litre jar kolkata" searches where national brands are absent and directories are beatable with depth + GBP. [O+H]
2. **Directories are the true local SERP gatekeepers.** A Justdial/Sulekha/IndiaMART presence with reviews is not optional for local commercial queries. [O]
3. **Zenith's regulatory content is an unexploited authority asset** competitors don't even attempt. Extend to video/test-report library. [F]
4. **The distributor journey is contested by fraudsters** (Bisleri's warning is the evidence) — a verifiable, transparent distributor page with company documents converts better than a form. [O]
5. **Kinley/Aquafina are non-entities in local delivery search** — comparison content should benchmark Bisleri primarily. [O]
