# 10 — Content and Topical Authority Strategy

Principle (per brief): content must serve real purchase decisions, not chase traffic. Zenith's proven authority wedge: **regulatory/water-quality education** — the IS 14543 article is the site's #4 page (5,267 impressions) and its zero-click diagnosis is a snippet opportunity, not a failure [F].

## 3. Internal linking specification (content → commercial)

Every Cluster A/B post links OUT to: the 20L page (household intent), the relevant city hub (local intent), and products (research intent) — 2–4 contextual links per post, descriptive anchors using the everyday-language register (§02.D). Commercial pages link back to the ONE most relevant guide (not all), keeping link equity focused. Hubs interlink per the post-relaunch architecture (protect it).

## 4. Format & evidence rules

- Byline with real author role; review date; sources cited (Tier-1 first — mirrors this research package's register).
- Lab/regulatory claims only against official documents (FSSAI/BIS); the BIS-mandatoryness conflict (Risk R1) resolved BEFORE publishing anything new on certification.
- No AI-generated filler: every post needs owner-verifiable substance.
- Publishing cadence: 2/month sustainable; quality bar over volume.

## 5. Content NOT to create

- Generic "benefits of drinking water" (low commercial value, saturated).
- City pages beyond genuine service area (policy).
- Comparison hit-pieces on named competitors beyond factual, sourced comparison.
- Any "best water brand in India" listicle without verifiable methodology.
