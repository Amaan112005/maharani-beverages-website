# 01 — Executive Strategy Report

**Client:** Maharani Beverages LLP (Zenith Water) · **Date:** 2026-10-31 · **Basis:** GSC export 29 Jul–28 Oct 2026 (871 clicks / 35,475 impressions / 2.46% CTR / pos ~8), post-launch technical state (deployed today), market/consumer/competitor research logged in deliverable 14.
**Evidence classes:** [F] fact · [O] observed · [R] research-backed · [H] hypothesis · [U] unknown.

---

## 1. Executive assessment

Zenith enters this programme from a position most local water brands never reach: **a technically clean, 487-page, fully indexable website with real commercial visibility** (the 20L page already earns 115 clicks/quarter at position 7) and a defensible authority wedge (regulatory education: 5,267 impressions on the IS 14543 article) that no competitor — national or local — contests [F/O].

The commercial problem is precise, not vague: **the site is seen plenty and clicked rarely.** Of 35,475 impressions, 32% sit in a near-me/local cluster converting at ~1.9%; a further 3,224 impressions across 55 queries convert at 0.19%; the highest-intent query class (pricing, 375 impressions) converted at zero through the entire window [F]. Meanwhile impressions themselves declined ~39% from August to October [F] — the residual of the July mass-expansion followed by Google's index-then-demote response, compounded by post-monsoon demand cooling [H, both mechanisms documented].

The strategy therefore is not "more pages" or "more traffic". It is: **convert the visibility already won, own the local surface competitors ignore, and turn regulatory authority into trust.**

The single most important number in this report: **recovering the existing zero-click pool to a modest 3% CTR ≈ +95 organic clicks per quarter at current positions — before any ranking work, before GBP, before GA4.** [F inputs, H arithmetic]

## 2. The decline, honestly explained (no false certainty)

Daily impressions: Aug 515 → Sep 327 → Oct 313. Daily clicks: 12.7 → 8.7 → 7.2 [F].
Two mechanisms, neither fully provable without server logs/live GSC:

1. **Index-then-demote on the July expansion** — 465 templated pages shipped 20 Jul; the August peak then eroded as thin-page quality was reassessed. The relaunch (shipped today) addresses the root causes: differentiation, link equity, depth, compliance. **[H, strongly consistent with timing]**
2. **Seasonality** — monsoon (Jul–Sep) is peak bottled-water demand in eastern India; October cools structurally. **[H, seasonal pattern]**

**Test:** if the relaunch is the dominant fix, November–December should stabilise above the October baseline despite seasonality; if seasonality dominates, expect a further modest dip then a pre-summer surge. Either way, the recovery actions in this package are justified on their own evidence.

## 3. Ten biggest ORGANIC GROWTH opportunities (ranked)

1. **Near-me cluster recovery** — 331 queries / 4,351 impressions / 84 clicks [F]. GBP + snippets + pincode-on-page.
2. **Zero-click striking-distance pool** — 55 queries / 3,224 impressions at 0.19% CTR ("water supply near me", "order water online 20l", "mineral water 20 litre"…) [F].
3. **Pricing-query class** — 375 impressions, 0 clicks; relaunch pricing visibility now live; first cohort to verify [F].
4. **Google Business Profile absence** — the map pack is the near-me SERP; Zenith isn't in it [O].
5. **Regulatory authority extension** — own "14543", TDS, label-reading, lab reports; zero competitor content [O].
6. **Comparison-query CTR** — "kinley vs bisleri" class: 267+ impressions, ~1 click; article exists, snippet/title under-sells [F].
7. **Merchant listings channel** — 27.4% CTR when shown; feed hygiene + GMC verification multiplies a proven channel [F].
8. **Desktop visibility gap** — pos 11.56 vs mobile 7.05 [F]; desktop content depth + CWV.
9. **Directory surface** — Justdial/Sulekha/IndiaMART own local commercial SERPs; NAP citations are free real estate [O].
10. **Subscription intent** — "water subscription" demand exists (82 impr, pos 15.7) with no landing page [F]; gated on fulfilment capability.

## 5. Ten biggest RISKS / weaknesses

1. **R1 — BIS/FSSAI public-claim conflict:** site FAQ says BIS "no longer mandatory"; multiple 2026 industry/regulatory sources say IS 14543 certification is legally required to sell packaged drinking water. One is wrong; must be resolved with counsel. **[U — P0]**
2. **Measurement blindness** until GA4 lands — growth work proceeds unmeasured; deferral respected, spec ready.
3. **Seasonal demand trough** misread as strategy failure (see §2).
4. **Review-markup absence** cedes snippet stars to any competitor with a real corpus.
5. **GBP absence** leaves near-me demand to directories and rivals indefinitely.
6. **Homepage LCP (~5.5s lab)** — slideshow reveal gates the brand's first impression.
7. **4 H1-overlap pairs** — contained cannibalisation; decision pending.
8. **Dependence on WhatsApp** as primary channel — policy/rate-limit/platform risk unmanaged.
9. **Thin-page quality reassessment** risk if locality pages aren't deepened where value exists.
10. **Owner-fact dependencies** (deposit policy, MOQ, SLAs, lab docs) — several P1s blocked until supplied.

## 6. Most important competitor insights

- **Bisleri is a brand fortress with a local-search moat of nothing:** no locality pages, no WhatsApp, no transparent pricing, app-only subscription [O]. Zenith should never fight the brand battle; it should own the service battle Bisleri structurally ignores.
- **The actual SERP competition is directories + informal locals** [O] — beatable with depth + GBP + reviews, not with more templated pages.
- **Kinley/Aquafina are irrelevant in local delivery search** [O] — comparison content benchmarks Bisleri only.
- **Bisleri's fake-distributorship warning proves the distributor journey is trust-contested** [O] — Zenith's transparent distributor page is a strategic asset.
- **The market is growing ~10%/yr** [R] — share is available without stealing demand.

## 7. Most important consumer insights

- **Taste + safety, not price, are the primaries** [R]; price-shoppers are a loud minority ("cheapest…" queries convert poorly anyway).
- **Income-independent safety concern** in West Bengal research [R] — mass-market positioning is valid; don't over-premium the brand voice.
- **The business is 100% local and word-of-mouth** [O] — the website's job is to make referral-verification instant (GBP + reviews + transparent pages), not to replace word of mouth.
- **Near-me is the discovery language** (331 queries) [F] — and it resolves to maps, not blue links.
- **Subscription inertia is the retention mechanism** — one subscription query outweighs ten one-off impressions in lifetime value [H].

## 8. Recommended strategic direction

**"The verifiable local water authority."** Position Zenith as the Kolkata-region supplier whose quality claims are provable (licences, lab reports, process transparency), whose locality coverage is real (pincode-verified), and whose regulatory education makes it the default trusted source — while national brands stay absent from local service search and local rivals stay invisible beyond directories. Every initiative in this package serves that position; anything that doesn't (mass pages, generic content, link schemes) is explicitly rejected.
