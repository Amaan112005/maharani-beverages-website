# 11 — Backlink and Digital Authority Strategy

**Limitation (stated):** no backlink crawler available (Ahrefs/Majestic/Moz absent). No profile claims are made in this package. The strategy below is opportunity-based, not profile-based. **[H/O]**, research date 2026-10-31.

---

## 1. Authority reality

- Zenith's brand-search demand is small (351 branded impressions/3 months [F]) → brand mentions are scarce; link earning must be asset-led.
- Assets that earn links genuinely: **regulatory explainers** (already proven: 5,267 impressions [F]), **lab-report library** (unique [O]), **the distributor transparency page** (fraud-aware audience [O]).
- Competitor benchmark: Bisleri's authority is brand-scale (130 plants, CSR programme [O]) — unmatchable directly; irrelevant to local link earning.

## 2. Opportunity channels (ranked by realism)

| # | Channel | Mechanism | Effort | Expected value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Citations/NAP** (Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Bing, Apple, Facebook) | Local trust + local pack eligibility | Low | High (P0 in deliverable 09) |
| 2 | **Local press** (Telegraph India, Times of India Kolkata, millenium post, local Bengali media) | Pitch: monsoon water-safety data story, packaged-water high-risk regulation consumer guide with founder quotes | Medium | High — local news links drive both referral and local organic |
| 3 | **Industry/trade press** (beverage/water industry publications, plant-economy sites) | Pitch: lab-report transparency initiative, distributor-economics data | Medium | Medium-high for B2B discovery |
| 4 | **Business associations** (BCCI — Bengal Chamber, CII eastern region, hotel/restaurant associations) | Membership + supplier directories + event presence | Medium | Medium; hospitality B2B trust |
| 5 | **Genuine partner/supplier links** (PET preform suppliers, logistics partners, packaging vendors' client pages) | Reciprocal real-world relationships | Low | Medium relevance |
| 6 | **Institutional relationships** (corporate clients' vendor/sustainability pages, school/hospital supplier disclosures) | Ask post-contract | Low | High trust, low volume |
| 7 | **Local event sponsorships** (marathons, community events where Zenith supplies water — water-partner credit pages) | Product-for-mention | Low | Local relevance + real brand |
| 8 | **Regulatory/consumer-resource links** (food-safety bloggers, consumer forums) from the verification guide | Asset naturally citeable | Low | Medium |

## 3. Explicitly rejected (per brief)

Purchased links · PBNs · mass directory submissions · fake press releases · fake reviews · irrelevant guest posts. Also: no "write for us" SEO-blog placements — zero relevance to water buyers.

## 4. Anchor and link-integrity rules

- Anchor text naturally mixed: brand, URL, generic ("their water quality guide"), occasional exact-match local (max ~10% exact commercial anchors).
- Every earned link pointed at the most relevant ASSET (guide/hub), not always homepage.
- Quarterly manual check of top referring pages (Search Console Links report — free and available once verified) for spam; disavow only clear toxicity.

## 5. Measurement (once GSC-verified)

- Referring domains trend (GSC Links), branded search volume trend in GSC (proxy for authority growth), referral sessions per channel (GA4 later). Targets: +10 referring domains/quarter from channels 2–8; branded impressions +50% in 2 quarters. **[H] targets, review quarterly.**
