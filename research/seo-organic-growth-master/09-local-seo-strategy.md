# 09 — Local SEO and Geographic Growth Strategy

Evidence: GSC [F], SERP observations [O], audits [F], operator economics research [R].

---

## 1. The local search reality (measured)

- **Near-me cluster: 331 queries, 4,351 impressions, 84 clicks, positions 5–8** [F] — Zenith's largest and most under-converted search class. Near-me SERPs are dominated by the **map pack**, which requires Google Business Profile — currently absent for the brand [O].
- Directories (Justdial/Sulekha/IndiaMART) hold top organic positions for local commercial queries in Kolkata [O, observed 2026-10-31].
- Mobile position 7.05 vs desktop 11.56 [F] — local intent skews mobile; site is mobile-strong post-relaunch.

## 2. P0 — Google Business Profile (the single highest-leverage local asset)

Spec (owner-executed; needs owner verification):
1. Create/claim GBP for Maharani Beverages LLP, Ranihati Industrial Park, Howrah (eligibility: genuine production facility = qualifies as a place of business).
2. Categories: Primary "Water supplier" (or "Drinking water supplier"); secondary "Water purification company", "Bottled water supplier".
3. Service area: Kolkata, Howrah, Hooghly, 24 Parganas, + genuine delivery cities (match the site's claimed coverage exactly — never beyond).
4. Products: all SKUs with prices (mirrors site pricing).
5. Photos: factory, fleet, jars, team (genuine only).
6. Review programme: ask every converted customer; respond to all. **This is the #1 ranking + trust lever for near-me.**
7. Q&A: seed with real FAQs (owner answers).
8. Website link → /areas-we-serve (hub).

Expected effect: map-pack presence for near-me queries converts independent of organic rank; CTR on the 4,351-impression near-me cluster is the recovery pool (§07).

## 3. Citations (NAP consistency)

Create/claim (name-address-phone EXACTLY as on site): Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Bing Places, Facebook Page, Google Business Profile, Apple Business Connect. Rationale: NAP inconsistency across these exact directories suppresses local rankings (multiple 2026 Indian local-SEO sources [R]); Zenith currently has no visible listings [O]. Budget: zero (self-serve).

## 4. Existing geographic architecture — honest evaluation

Post-relaunch: 485 URLs, ≤5-click depth, no islands, existence-guarded links [F]. The pages are differentiated at the sentence level (5-word-shingle overlap 0.58–0.64 within families) but share vocabulary — acceptable for geo-programmes, improvable where value justifies it.

| Page class | Count | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| City family (core 3 families × 8 cities) | 24 | **Protect + improve** — real impressions (e.g., durgapur/asansol pages already earn clicks) |
| Locality (3 families × 65 localities) | 195 | **Differentiate progressively** — add locality-specific facts (landmarks, delivery windows, depot routes) where operations confirm; leave the rest stable |
| Industry × city | ~100 | **Retain** — long-tail B2B (hotels/hospitals/schools in each city) |
| City hubs | 8 | **Improve** — hub titles/CTAs per §07; hubs now carry segment links |

**Rules (from brief):** never publish a location the business can't serve; never invent local facts; consolidation (not expansion) for anything underperforming after 2 quarters.

## 5. Deepening local relevance (quality, not volume)

1. **Local proof blocks**: per city page — delivery areas list, typical delivery window, local contact macro, 1–2 genuine locality details (e.g., "daily route through Sector V" for Salt Lake). Source: operations team [U → owner input].
2. **Local reviews embedded** (with consent) once the GBP programme produces them.
3. **Local imagery**: real delivery photos per city (unique image per hub).
4. **City-level GBP posts** (offers, monsoon advisories) — fresh signal.

## 6. Coverage honesty rule (standing)

Expansion into a new city requires: delivery capability confirmation → THEN page improvement → THEN citation/GBP service-area update. Search volume never justifies a location. [Policy]

## 7. Measurement

- GBP insights (calls, direction requests, website clicks) monthly.
- GSC near-me cluster CTR + position (baseline stored: 84/4351, pos 5–8).
- Pincode-checker result distribution (delivers real demand geography once GA4 lands).
- Directory listing impressions (Justdial dashboard).
